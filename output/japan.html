Data Quirks & Release Discipline数据特性与发布纪律

Japan's national accounts are revised heavily between the first preliminary and the second preliminary QoQ prints, and headline CPI is dominated by fresh-food and energy swings. The dashboard keeps FRED/OECD, IMF SDMX, and BIS provenance explicit so revision behaviour can be traced back to the compiling agency.日本国民账户在一次速报与二次速报之间修订幅度较大，总体CPI又高度受生鲜食品与能源波动影响。页面明确标注FRED/OECD、IMF SDMX与BIS的数据来源，以便把修订行为追溯到编制机构。

Prices & Costs价格与成本

IMF-compiled national CPI gives headline year-over-year and month-over-month inflation and the price level itself, which is the reproducible public path now that OECD's Japan CPI mirror is discontinued. The Statistics Bureau's own e-Stat API supplies the BoJ's actual policy reference measures natively: core CPI ex-fresh-food, core-core ex-fresh-food-and-energy, and the goods/services split, each published as year-over-year directly rather than derived on this page. The COICOP division breakdown (food, housing, transport, and the other CP01-CP12 categories) is not yet wired and remains a gap.IMF汇编的日本CPI提供总体同比、环比与价格水平本身。在OECD日本CPI镜像停更后，这是可复跑的公开路径。总务省统计局自有的e-Stat接口原生提供日银真正的政策参考口径：剔除生鲜食品的核心CPI、剔除生鲜食品与能源的核心核心CPI，以及商品/服务拆分，均由官方直接公布同比，而非本页派生。COICOP大类分项（食品、住房、交通等CP01-CP12类别）尚未接入，仍列为缺口。
