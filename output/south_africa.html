Data Quirks & Release Discipline数据特性与发布纪律

South African labour data comes from the quarterly QLFS, whose headline unemployment rate excludes discouraged workers reported separately in the expanded definition. Activity data is monthly but thin, and load-shedding breaks make several production series non-comparable across years. SARB's own web API is preferred for rates and prices because it is the compiling authority.南非劳动力数据来自季度QLFS，其官方失业率不含在扩大口径中单独统计的沮丧工人。活动数据为月度但覆盖较薄，限电断点使部分生产序列跨年不可比。利率与价格优先使用SARB自有Web API，因其为编制机构。
Official chart series for this section are currently in development.本分析模块的官方指标序列正在标准化接入中。
